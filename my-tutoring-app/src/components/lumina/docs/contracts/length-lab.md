### R2 — every mode's levers change the picture, never the answer · OBSERVED (2026-10-09)
- **Property:** `lengthLabLevers.ts` declares levers on every mode, and every catalog miss is answered by a lever on every item (no `unanswered`). Help levers draw a picture (word model, end lines, unit marks, object outline, unit model, order steps, chain model); models outside the item never use an item object's name or unit; no lever text or scene fact carries a digit (`leverTextLeaks`). Simplify (far_pair, shorter_object, far_three; none on indirect) stays in the mode, gets its own `~simpler` id, never repeats the learner's object, length, count or units (`simplerLeaks`), and is ungraded; the full item comes back blank. A pull that changes nothing is refused (already pulled); object_outline is not offered on an estimate before its guess (since 2026-10-19 the lever set's `offer`, before a refusal in the pull). Since 2026-10-19 `unit_model` and `chain_model` are model levers and `word_model` a meaning lever: the set's `support` fact says when the model comes by the item's tier (easy before a try, medium after a miss, hard never before a try). The tier's unit marks and fit line stay starting positions; `unit_marks` is offered only where the session hides them.

### R3 — compare and indirect take the answer spoken or tapped · OBSERVED (2026-10-13)
- **Property:** a compare or indirect challenge is a SAY_OR_SHOW item (`response: 'speech'` with `acceptsGesture`, built by `spokenBoss`). Re-based 2026-10-19: the tutor is asked in the buttons' words, built in code (`lengthAsk`): compare "Is the A longer or shorter than the B, or are they the same length?", indirect "Which is longer, the A or the B? Or are they the same length?". The key is a menu the ask says in full (`lengthKey`: "longer" / "shorter" / "the same" about the first object; the object's name or "the same" on indirect), with the sentence forms ("the pencil is longer") accepted, and `misses` from the card's list (`reversed`, `said_same`, `missed_same`; `chose_shorter`, `said_same`). The opposite word, or naming the other object as longer, is not credited. A credited spoken answer records the result once and shows the credited choice; a tap still ends any pending spoken answer and is checked by the activity; a correct tap is recorded once. The scene's constraints withhold which object is longer (the doctrine says the learner may say or tap). tile_and_count and estimate_then_tile (checked on the tiles laid), two_unit_compare (its question opens only after both measurements) and order (several answers) stay gesture-only.
- **Demanded by:** qa/workspace-rollout/ROLLOUT.md VB-1 (voice beside the tap).
