**Why nothing shrank — the mechanism, not the symptom.** `target_primitive` is a
**stored field on the published subskill**, not a runtime resolution. Shipping
`word-sorter` (GAP-010) and `sentence-builder` (GAP-012) added *capability* but
retargeted *no curriculum row*. The demand number is therefore insensitive to
primitive builds by construction — it moves only when a draft is edited and
published. Any future session that expects this count to fall because a primitive
shipped is reading the wrong instrument.

**The band correction that reframes the whole lane: this is a Kindergarten
problem.** Of 138, **131 are Kindergarten**, 7 are Grade 1, and **0 are Grade 2**.
So the pre-reader contract (`/reader-fit`) governs essentially every item in this
lane — picture-primary faces, nothing load-bearing in text, 🔊 read-aloud via
catalog `aiDirectives`. The handoff's house rule is not an edge case here; it is
the default case.

## Phase 1 — fit-first triage (grammar family, all 50)

Order of preference per the handoff: **route → extend → birth.**

### The anti-duplication finding — `spatial-scene` already exists

The handoff's headline predicted birth was "**Preposition/Spatial Scene** (~7
subskills): the child ENACTS 'the bear is UNDER the table' by dragging the bear".

**That primitive is already built.** `spatial-scene` — `catalog/math.ts:3743`,
component `primitives/visual-primitives/math/SpatialScene.tsx`, generator
`service/math/gemini-spatial-scene.ts`, registered in `primitiveRegistry.tsx`,
`evaluation/index.ts`, `mathGenerators.ts`. It is not a catalog phantom, and it is
not L0 — it carries 4 eval modes (`identify` β1.0 / `place` β2.0 / `describe` β3.0
/ `follow_directions` β4.0), support tiers with an answer-leak guard, and
`GenerationContext`-native scope threading.

It was invisible to the 07-04 map because it is filed under **math** (K.G.1
positional language), not literacy. The manifest generator consumes
`UNIVERSAL_CATALOG` (`catalog/index.ts:62`) — a flat concatenation with **no
subject partition** — so an LA objective *can* reach it. Reachability is confirmed
architecturally; whether the curator actually picks it for an LA preposition ask
is a runtime question, probed separately (see "Open probe" below).

**Verdict for the preposition cluster: EXTEND, not BIRTH.** Birthing a
Preposition/Spatial Scene primitive would have duplicated a mature K-1 primitive —
exactly what the fit-first gate exists to prevent. This is the single most
valuable finding of Phase 1.

**But it does not serve the LA demand as-shipped.** Reading the generator, the
gap is precise and mechanical:

- `VALID_POSITIONS` (`gemini-spatial-scene.ts:146`) admits 11 words: above, below,
  beside, left_of, right_of, between, on, under, next_to, in_front_of, behind.
- The prompt's band window (`SHARED_CONTEXT:166`) narrows that hard:
  *"Position words for K grade: **ONLY** above, below, beside, next_to"*;
  Grade 1 adds left_of/right_of.
- The grid-semantics block (`:168-173`) defines only above / below / left_of /
  right_of / beside / next_to. **`between`, `on`, `under`, `in_front_of`, and
  `behind` have no defined mapping to the 3×3 grid** — the LLM may emit them but
  is never told how to place them, so correctness is unowned.

The LA curriculum names exactly the words that fall in that hole:
LA004-01-F is *"Apply prepositions (**in, on, under, between**)"* and LA004-05-C
is *"above/below, **in front/behind**"*. At K the generator permits **none of
them**. `in` is not in `VALID_POSITIONS` at all, and direction/path words
(**through, around, across** — LA004-05-H) are absent by design: a 3×3 static grid
has no path semantics.

### Per-subskill verdicts — LA004 (50)

| Verdict | n | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **ROUTE** — served today, curriculum re-target only | 11 | draft-first: edit draft → `lineage-check` → publish |
| **EXTEND** — existing primitive, missing mode/band/vocab | 19 | `/add-eval-modes` + band work |
| **BIRTH** — genuinely new interaction | 4 | Sentence Mechanic (mechanics repair) |
| **TIER-3 / oral production** — designed tutor, not a primitive | 16 | keep on tutor by design |

**ROUTE (11) — `word-sorter` serves these at K today.** Critically, `word-sorter`
is *already* pre-reader-correct: at K the generator **requires** an emoji on every
word card and every bucket (`gemini-word-sorter.ts:196-200, 243-247`, reader-fit
RF-4), and the catalog carries three `aiDirectives` making "say the sort and each
word card aloud" the mandatory first tutor action, with a K band floor that never
withdraws word-voicing even at support tier `hard`. The handoff's predicted work
item — *"word-sorter grammar modes at K need picture-primary cards + read-aloud"* —
**is already done**. This is a closure, not a task.

- `binary_sort` (β1.5): LA004-01-A (noun/verb), LA004-01-D (common/proper),
  LA004-03-B (singular/plural — named verbatim in the catalog), LA004-03-E (a/an),
  LA004-03-J (definite/indefinite), LA004-04-C (he/she), LA004-05-I
  (location/direction prepositions — named verbatim), LA004-06-C (action/linking),
  LA004-06-F (present/past -ed)
- `ternary_sort` (β2.5): LA004-06-J (past/present/future — named verbatim)
- LA004-02-A (subject vs action in spoken sentences)

*Caveat recorded, not waved:* LA004-01-D (common vs proper nouns) sorts on a
**print** feature — the capital letter — which a pre-reader cannot perceive and an
emoji cannot encode. It is listed as ROUTE because the sort shape fits, but it
carries a reader-fit risk and should be `/reader-fit`-checked before its
re-target, not routed blind.

**EXTEND (19).** Two distinct extension jobs:

1. **`spatial-scene` preposition vocabulary + grid semantics (7).**
   LA004-01-F, LA004-05-B, -05-C, -05-D, -05-E, -05-F, -05-H. The demand is for
   containment/support/frontality prepositions (in, on, under, in front of,
   behind, between) and a path class (through, around, across) the current K
   window and grid semantics exclude. This is the pilot — see below.
2. **`word-sorter` K picture-pair mode (3).** LA004-03-C (singular↔plural picture
   pairs), LA004-03-G (irregular plurals mouse/mice), LA004-06-G (irregular past
   run/ran). All three are *pair-matching*, and `match_pairs` (β3.5) is
   **Grade 1+ only** by an explicit band floor — "text-to-text matching requires
   decoding". The floor is correct for word↔word; a **picture↔picture** pair mode
   would clear it at K without weakening it. `/add-eval-modes`.
3. **`sentence-builder` at K (9).** LA004-02-B/G/H, LA004-03-D/H, LA004-04-F/I,
   LA004-01-E, LA004-01-I. Built for grades 1-6 with text tiles; K needs
   picture-bearing tiles and read-aloud. Larger job than it looks — deferred.

**BIRTH (4) — Sentence Mechanic.** LA004-02-D (complete vs fragment), -02-E
(ending punctuation . ? !), -02-F (question word order + punctuation), plus
LA004-01-L (mixed grammar review) as a possible rider. `sentence-builder` BUILDS
sentences; nothing in the catalog **repairs** them. Demand confirmed at 4 — real,
but smaller than the preposition cluster, so it is not the pilot.

**TIER-3 / oral production (16).** LA004-01-C/H, -02 tail, -03-F/I/K, -04-A/B/G/H/J,
-06-A/D/E/H/I. "Use X in guided speaking/storytelling/conversation" — the
modality genuinely *is* conversation. Per the 07-04 map's Tier 3, these want a
**designed** Conversation Studio, not the generic fallback, and not a forced
tap-primitive. Left on `ai-tutor-session` deliberately; recorded as a closure with
a named reason, not a skip.

## Phase 2 pilot — selected

**`spatial-scene` preposition extension (7 subskills).**

Selected on demand, not cheapness ([[worked-primitives-self-select]]): it is the
largest cluster whose verdict requires work (7 vs Sentence Mechanic's 4 vs
picture-pairs' 3), it is the handoff's named pilot candidate, and the fit-first
gate converted it from a ~2-day birth into a bounded extension of a primitive that
already has eval modes, support tiers, and ctx-native scope.

It also satisfies direct-manipulation-first: `place` and `follow_directions`
already have the child **tap the grid to enact** the relation, which is the
interaction the handoff wanted a birth for.

Contract-first is REQUIRED and adds work here: `docs/contracts/spatial-scene.md`
does not exist, and the primitive has a live **math K.G.1** consumer whose
above/below/beside/next_to behavior must not be ablated by an LA-driven vocabulary
change. Derive the contract before editing (`/primitive-contract`), then
`--check` the edit.

### Open probe (blocking the pilot's first line of code)

Architectural reachability ≠ actual routing. Before extending vocabulary, confirm
by runtime trace whether a K LA preposition objective **selects** `spatial-scene`
at all:

- if it routes → the fix is vocabulary + grid semantics (the analysis above)
- if it does not route → the first defect is *discoverability* (a math-framed
  catalog `description`/`constraints` that an LA curator will not pick), and that
  fix precedes any vocabulary work

`manifestOnly` trace via `POST /api/lumina/topic-trace` on the published
LA004-05-B / LA004-01-F objective text. **Not yet run** — dev server was down at
census time and is starting.

## What this census does NOT claim

- No runtime probe of `word-sorter` actually producing a clean grammar sort at K
  has been run in this slice; the ROUTE verdicts rest on a code read of the
  generator's K emoji requirement and the catalog's K band floor, plus the
  primitive's batch-2 support-tier wiring. Each re-target should carry an
  `/eval-test` before its draft edit is published.
- No curriculum draft has been edited. Every ROUTE verdict is a *recommendation*;
  the draft-first pipeline (draft → `lineage-check` → publish) has not been
  entered, and `curriculum_published` was read-only throughout.
- The 88 non-grammar items (LA005/LA006/LA003/LA007/LA001) are rostered but **not
  triaged** — this lane is scoped to grammar first.
