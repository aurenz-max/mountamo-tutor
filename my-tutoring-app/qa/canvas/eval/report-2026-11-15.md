## Probe and script fixes at the class

- **Ready reads:** a ready read on the current hash now outranks a major probe finding (never a blocking one). Before, `statusOf` kept thermometer-zones and night-sky-orbit at heal even after a ready read.
- **verdict-colour:** a straight `<polyline>` has a 0-height box and was counted invisible. ring-the-tens' miss rings were missed, which made a false verdict-colour finding.
- **dim-text:** words are now measured against what is drawn behind them, not the first svg rect. 4 batches reported this on about 12 canvases.
- **stage-colour:** skips rects inside `<defs>`/`<clipPath>`, and the skill notes that full-scene worlds are fine.
- **Keycap emoji:** keycap emoji ("1️⃣") are no longer read as words.
- **control-overlap:** a new probe, which missed pizza-share's buttons before. It is minor and checked at rest only: placed things and terminals overlap by design.
- **Earlier today:** small-text and monkey thresholds, height severity, a 44px rounding fix, a retry when the gate fails on a Windows file lock, and the down-vote hold.
