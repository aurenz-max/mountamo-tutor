## Finding that changes rung (2) — `count_sides` / `count_corners` have a MEASURED home

The ladder's next rung was queued on the assumption that side/corner counting belongs
somewhere. It does, precisely, and at **two** grades:

- **G1 `GEOM001-01-b`** @ 0.785 — *"**Count the number of sides and vertices** of various
  2D shapes to confirm their classification."* Its own examples enumerate
  triangle (3,3), square (4,4), rectangle (4,4), trapezoid (4,4), hexagon (6,6),
  circle (0,0), pentagon (5,5), rhombus (4,4) — **eight of the nine shapes in
  di-shapes' Fork A menu**, with the same counts the pack would judge.
- **K `GEOM001-02-A`** @ 0.786 — *"Identify and name basic 2D shapes based on their
  attributes (**sides and vertices**)."*

So `count_sides`/`count_corners` are not a G1-only extension: K has an attribute-based
naming subskill that the modes serve directly. Answer set is 0/3/4/5/6 — inside the
`#46`-benched spoken-number-word class, so **no new bench sitting** (standing gate 1 clear).

## ⚠️ Blocker to clear IN the L1 slice — the catalog fences the modes off

`catalog/di.ts:456` `constraints` currently reads:

> *"…**no side/corner counting tasks yet**, and no composing or comparing shapes; use a
> geometry primitive with those modes when counting or composing IS the objective."*

That is a correct L0 birth fence, but it is **manifest-visible steering**. Shipping
`count_sides`/`count_corners` without rewriting that clause leaves the manifest actively
routing counting objectives *away* from the primitive that just gained the modes — the
new modes would be born unreachable. Lift the counting clause; **keep** the 3D-solids and
composing clauses (see below).

## Keep the other fences — the near-misses prove they are load-bearing

At G1 the 4th and 5th nearest subskills are things di-shapes deliberately does **not** do:

- `GEOM001-02-a` @ 0.770 — *"Identify common 3D shapes… cube, cone, cylinder, sphere"*
- `GEOM001-03-c` @ 0.769 — *"Select and position pattern blocks to create a composite shape"*

Both sit only ~0.03 below the top-1, i.e. comfortably above τ. The `constraints` exclusions
("FLAT 2D shapes only — NO 3D solids", "no composing") are what stop a 3D-solids or
pattern-block objective from landing on a primitive that draws flat SVG shapes and judges a
spoken name. Do not relax them while lifting the counting clause.

## Probe defect found and fixed in this slice (the verdict above is post-fix)

`scripts/curriculum_fit_probe.py:85` resolved the subject with
`subject_for_domain(args.domain)`. The live path
(`submission_service.py:461` → `CurriculumMappingService.resolve_by_retrieval`) uses
`subject_for_primitive(primitive, domain)`, which consults `_PRIMITIVE_TO_SUBJECT` FIRST.
For the two overridden DI packs the two disagree, so the probe violated the skill's Key
Rule 4 ("match the live verdict").

**Reproduced before fixing.** Pre-fix, `--primitive di-shapes --domain di` scoped to
**LANGUAGE_ARTS** and returned `ABSTAIN [diffuse]`, best 0.722, coherent 2/5, ranking a
geometry primitive against **Rhyme Recognition, Onset-Rime Blending and Phoneme Isolation** —
verbatim the misattribution class `/curriculum-fit` was created to catch
(`QA_curriculum_mapping_misattribution.md`: *"a math primitive was being attributed to a
phonics skill"*). A session that trusted that output would have filed a false curriculum gap
for K geometry.

The gap was **masked, not absent**: the `di-math-facts` 2026-07-24 report records running the
probe with a deliberately wrong `--domain math` to force the right scope. The workaround was
documented in the report but never in the script, so it depended on the operator knowing.
Meanwhile `curriculum_retrieval_service.py:62-66` already claimed the override *"removes the
`--domain literacy` workaround for probes/attribution"* — true for the service, never true for
the probe script.

**Fixed** in `curriculum_fit_probe.py` (primitive-first resolution) and in
`curriculum_fit_sweep.py`, which had the same defect plus a structural one: it hoisted
`subject` and `grades` out of the per-primitive loop, so `/curriculum-fit di` would have swept
*every* DI pack under one subject. Subject is now resolved per primitive with a
per-subject grade cache, and each row records the subject it was scored against.

**Controls (static resolution, no embeddings):** only the two overridden primitives move —
`di-shapes` and `di-math-facts` → MATHEMATICS; `di-letter-sounds` / `di-word-reading` /
`di-sentence-reading` stay LANGUAGE_ARTS; `ten-frame`→MATHEMATICS, `rhyme-studio`→LANGUAGE_ARTS,
`dna-explorer`→SCIENCE unchanged; cross-cutting `core` still declines (`None`). `py_compile` clean
on both scripts.

**Worth a follow-up, not fixed here:** `scripts/verify_retrieval_matcher.py:49` and
