## How the families were sorted

Each mode was read from its assignment builder, how the component commits an answer, and the catalog guidance (four read-only passes, one per quarter of the list). Codes: **S** spoken, tutor-judged; **T** one tap, activity-checked; **SG** spoken with a gesture accepted (`response: 'speech', acceptsGesture: true`); **B** board (build, draw, type, several moves, or a tap followed by a Check press, which `tapBoss` does not allow).

**Update 10-19 (framework review): one rotation, every variant on the card.** The rotation is the same for every boss: ask one item, the learner answers, the judge the card names checks it, the host retries or advances, levers help. What differs per mode is a card value (`answer`, `judge`, `flow`, `moves`), and every item's assignment now comes from one function (`bossAssignment`: a spoken key, or none for a gesture the activity checks). So none of B-E needs a new preset or bespoke wiring:

| Bucket | Families | Card value (per mode) | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Every mode S | 17 | `SPOKEN` | `spokenBoss` |
| B. Every mode SG | 14 | `SAY_OR_SHOW` (`judge: 'either'`, sets `acceptsGesture`) | `spokenBoss` |
| C. SG plus B or T modes | 24 | `SAY_OR_SHOW` / `TAP_ONE` / board, per mode | `spokenBoss`: `key` null on shown items, `show` plays them, `modeOf` picks SAY_OR_SHOW |

Per-item splits (fast-fact, fact-file, knowledge-check, place-value-chart, timeline-explorer) are the same mechanism: an item with no key is shown. What stays per boss is content, not wiring: `validate`, `items`, `ask`, the key, the levers and the harness's moves. Guards: `boss.test.ts` "one spoken boss mixes said, shown and say-or-show items", "a say-or-show mode beside other modes needs modeOf", "say-or-show and a tap with a commit press are card values".

Left on the framework: `spokenBoss` now carries shown and board items too, so it is the rotation preset under a narrow name. Renaming it (and deriving `preset` from the card instead of storing it) lets boards use it as well and leaves `defineBoss` for nothing; that is a rename across the 14 boss files, done when wave 5 starts.
