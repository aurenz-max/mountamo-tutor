# You & Me support tiers — 2026-09-07

Support withdrawal is implemented for both evaluation modes, including blended and mixed sessions.

| Tier | Visual support | Preparation and tutor help |
|---|---|---|
| easy | Speaker highlight and actor/object marker | Role checklist; independent-action mode also checks the no-help referent |
| medium | Speaker highlight; actor marker withdrawn | One short mode-specific perspective reminder |
| hard | Highlight and actor marker withdrawn | No preparation text; help invites another look without walking through roles |

Names, role labels, named scene, required task, microphone controls, scene replay and post-error correction remain available at every tier. No scored explanation is added: that would change the spoken-production contract. Neither targets nor model sentences appear in preparation. Existing saved payloads without a tier retain their original behavior.

The interaction combines spoken sentence production with role tracking. Perception and instructional support are the useful levers. Worked examples and recognition choices would expose answers or change the task; removing scene access would impair accessibility. Structural complexity is a separate follow-up.

Implementation: `youAndMeSupport.ts` defines shared mode-aware scaffolds and tutor reveal policies. The generator applies support fields to every challenge after scene fallback and role assignment. `YouAndMe.tsx` consumes the visual fields, while `youAndMeScript.ts` uses the same preparation in audible opening/replay prompts and passes tier/policy through live context and item cues. The catalog gives that policy precedence over generic hints. The tutor-test route now forwards its optional difficulty parameter.

Harness adaptation: this context-native generator uses the existing strictly normalized `GenerationContext.supportTier`, already resolved from manifest config.difficulty. No second raw config/normalizer was introduced. There was no numeric-difficulty wiring to remove. Tier application never alters scene content, roles, counts or answer derivation.

Validation:

- 48 focused tests pass, including rendered withdrawal, retained correction/task access, both-mode/blended fallback invariance and existing tutoring audits.
- Nine real eval-test generations / 58 turns pass: both modes at easy/medium/hard, mixed hard, blended easy, and a hard pinned personal-pronoun task with conflicting third-person/possessive intent. Inspected every turn's support fields and fixed-scene role pairs. All used generated scene content.
- Three real tutor prompt probes (easy/medium/hard) pass with zero audit findings; all 11 context keys resolve, including the selected tier and reveal policy.
- Full project TypeScript comparison: zero new diagnostic lines versus the pre-change baseline. Lumina typecheck: zero errors.
- Raw evidence: `you-and-me-l3-*.json` in this directory.
