# Ramp Lab investigation build — 2026-09-10

Adds `plan_fair_test` and `explain_from_trials` to the existing engineering primitive.
The learner edits a controlled comparison or receives one, predicts, runs two animated
force trials, and records the evidence. Explanation mode then judges a spoken comparison
against those actual simulated records. [Learning contract](../../src/components/lumina/docs/contracts/ramp-lab.md).

## Implementation

- Two new catalog modes, beta priors .75 and 2.5, mirrored in the backend. Existing
  three priors and default discrimination `(a=1.4, c=0)` verified unchanged.
- Code-owned pool, one-variable plan validation, immutable trial records, and
  round-robin blend selection. Each new mode has 18 setups across surface/angle/mass.
- Context-native generator consumes explicit mode, objective/intent, grade, and
  support tier. Registration passes the full context. New-mode descriptions are
  code-owned to prevent a generated physics primer leaking the explanation.
- Shared judged speech runner with `concept_statement`, 1200 ms silence close,
  two-correction cap, and tutor context. No additional audio engine or response class.
- Separate plan, prediction, and explanation evidence through existing submission.
  The tester now forwards its evaluation callback and offers all five task choices.

tutor with synthetic learner text; they do not exercise a child's microphone.

or **Explain from Trials** in **Ramp task**. Example topic: “Investigate how surface
friction changes the push needed to move a box.” Use an elementary/Grade 3 profile.
