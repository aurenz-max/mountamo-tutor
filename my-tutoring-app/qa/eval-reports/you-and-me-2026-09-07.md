## Contract checks

- **G1:** All session and challenge fields consumed by `YouAndMe.tsx` and `youAndMeScript.ts` are present. Every draw has six `describe_action` turns, distinct partner names and populated person/object emoji. The component handles this core type through its shared spoken runner.
- **G2:** Required flat generation fields reconstruct two participants and complete actions for all twelve scenes. No nullable or missing reconstruction observed.
- **G4:** Visible named actor, completed action and highlighted named speaker determine the answer. Each pair preserves participants, actor, object and action and reverses speaker. Every draw requires exactly three I and three you responses; actor positions vary. Model responses derive from actor/speaker equality, with no independent answer key to desynchronize.
- **Answer leak:** Initial UI and spoken stimulus give the named scene and named speaking role, without a model I/you sentence. Hear-scene repeats that stimulus. Expected pronoun/model live only in silent judge instructions; spoken modeling is restricted to correction after an attempt. The judge contract refuses swapped perspectives, name echoes, bare pronouns and token-presence matching. Source checks establish intended behavior, not live model compliance.
- **G5:** No fallback scenes were indicated in the four sampled responses (0/12). The source rejects malformed/duplicate scenes, retries once, then selects a distinct built-in familiar scene and reports fallback use in the description/log. Reviewed built-ins remain solvable and paired; fallback does not preserve a narrowly constrained custom theme, so the sampled general-routine result should not be generalized to topic-fidelity guarantees.

Sample scenes: washed the apple; caught the red/green ball; stacked the blocks; washed the red paintbrush. All require the same pronoun-perspective task.
