## How Pip behaves

Two shared hooks carry almost every integration; policies and per-primitive targets are in `src/components/lumina/pip/README.md`.

- **Classic primitives** (`useWorkspacePipSurface`, 112 component files): a dock above one `workspace` region that wraps the item's model and its answer controls. Pip outlines the region only while the tutor speaks on this block and item, looks at whatever the child presses inside it, and celebrates only the primitive's own confirmed result. It never rings a single control, so no answer choice is singled out. Components with no tutor hook never point.
- **Judged primitives with one question panel** (`useStimulusPipSurface`, 18 component files): Pip outlines the stimulus (the texts, the scene, the dice, the rule and case, the story), never the answer menu; it receives a hands answer only while it is judged (a phrase plan, a big-amount placement, a built chain) and celebrates the held reveal.
- Hand-wired variants: Practice Problem (dock in the header so it holds through the reveal overlay; the drawing is received while judged), Image Panel and Blueprint Canvas (the graded work is received), Distribution Explorer and Reading Repair Studio (no celebration: guided exploration and provisional verdicts are not confirmed results), Figurative Language Finder (no surface in find and review, where a look at a tapped plain span would mark the figurative ones).
