## Reading (judged 2026-09-15)

**Agreement is low because Gemini's picks are 46% generic scaffolds, and TypeSafe cannot pick a
container by semantic match.** Of 175 Gemini picks, 81 have fewer than two eval modes (explainers,
cards, DI spoken practice, take-home). Eight of them account for ~80 of the ~101 "Gemini-only" picks:
di-spoken-practice 15, comparison-panel 13, concept-card-grid 12, fast-fact 11, annotated-example 8,
take-home-activity 8, foundation-explorer 6, how-it-works 4. The curator picks these by POLICY (the
Introduce / Visualize / Apply ladder, spoken-first for explain verbs, caregiver block last); their
catalog descriptions name no topic, so a "which component teaches this" Choice gives them ~0
probability whatever the instruction says. The affordance-filtered introduce Choice (22 options)
lifts generic hit@roles from 17% to 30%; that is the ceiling of this question shape.

**On specialists TypeSafe is often right where it disagrees.** "Model two-digit numbers using tens
rods and ones units" → TypeSafe base-ten-blocks (fit 2.70) vs Gemini place-value-chart; "Compare two
identical containers with different amounts" → measure-lab (2.86), Gemini skipped it. But it
over-weights the lesson topic against the objective even in the objective-focused arm: "Identify
geometric shapes" inside a structures subskill → shape-strength-tester (2.77) over shape-sorter.
The objective-focus arm moved nothing measurable (28% → 28%, 76% mode agreement).

**Eval-mode agreement 74–76% on 41 shared primitives; disagreements lean harder.** TypeSafe picks
multi_step over represent (tape-diagram), story_mountain over bme (story-map), classify over
measure. The resolver sees the whole lesson and the block's siblings; TypeSafe sees one objective.
Not a replacement for resolveLessonEvalModes on this evidence.

**Cost and speed.** Affordance filter: 68k → 38k input tokens and 966 → 600 ms per objective. Gemini
brief + manifest + resolver: 10.9 s per subskill. TypeSafe per subskill (3 objectives, parallel): ~1 s.
Pricing still unverified.

**Where it could earn a place (none of these are built):**
1. *Specialist shortlist into the curator prompt* — TypeSafe's top-5 specialists per objective as one
   suggestion line, the skill-suggestion cookbook pattern (the agent keeps judgment; wrong loads
   halved there). Touches the manifest prompt, so it needs the "never add curator rules" ruling read
   against it first.
2. *No-specialist detector* — when the objective's best fit is < 1.5 the catalog has no specialist and
   deep-dive / generic is correct; a cheap, code-owned signal.
3. *Not* the eval-mode resolver, *not* the whole manifest.
