**The curator's own variance is the dominant effect.** Two control runs on identical fixed
objectives share only 44–49% of their picks (Jaccard). The suggestion block moves the pick set by
the same amount, so at one run per arm the aggregate cannot separate the block from noise. The one
signal above noise is take rate: the curator included a shown candidate 46–50% of the time on its
own and 56–64% with the block. That is a modest nudge, not a redirection — the same ratio the
skill-suggestion cookbook reports (wrong loads halved, not eliminated).

**Loose arm: real wins and real harm.** Wins where a specialist replaced a panel: measure-lab for
"compare two containers", evidence-finder for "identify the claim" and "explain how evidence
strengthens", shape-builder for "identify right/acute/obtuse triangles", word-builder for the
prefix/suffix objectives. Harm where a 1.5–1.9 candidate displaced the right tool: dump-truck-loader
over measure-lab, custom-visual over light-shadow-lab — though the second also happened in a
CONTROL rerun, so part of that harm is the curator's own noise.

**Strict arm: safe, smaller.** No displacement cases. The curator took base-ten-blocks for "tens
rods and ones units", bar-model for "compare the counts", shape-builder for triangle types, and it
correctly REJECTED strong-fit candidates whose verb did not match: sorting-station (2.6) for
"identify clues", light-shadow-lab (2.9) for "explain the rule" (spoken-first explain kept),
evidence-finder (2.4) for "identify the main claim". Candidates reach only 55% of objectives, which
is the price of the fit floor.

**What this does not measure.** Whether the changed blocks teach better. Take rate and specialist
share are selection facts; the quality judgment lives in the lesson-coverage judge and costs full
generation. Adopting the strict block on the production prompt is a quality call, not a selection
call — and with the curator varying 50% run to run on its own, a judged study needs several runs
per arm to say anything.

**If pursued:** (1) run the lesson-coverage judge on 6–8 subskills × 3 runs × {control, strict};
(2) independently, the 44–49% run-to-run variance on fixed objectives is a finding about the curator
itself and is probably the larger lever.
