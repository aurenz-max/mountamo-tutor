## Verdict on the old screen, and what changed
The self-check had a real code check (one tap against `correctIndex`), so the primitive was bindable, but the modes
did not differ on screen and two things undercut the check:
- **The eval-mode pin did nothing.** The generator constrained the self-check `difficulty` field to the mode's
  challenge types (`recall_easy`...), which the validator does not accept, so every question came out `medium`. The
  component showed the same screen for all three modes.
- **The answers stayed on screen.** The key stats row stayed visible during the questions, so any key-stat question
  in recall or apply was read off the screen. The score gave 40% for opening tabs.
- **Gemini picked the key and its options.** The right option was often the longest, a few stems printed their key,
  and a short pool was padded with "Option A..D" placeholder questions.

Rebuilt (`factFileWorkspace.ts`, `gemini-fact-file.ts` `pickSelfChecks`):
- **explore**: the file stays open above the question (key stats and tabs); the learner looks the answer up.
- **recall / apply**: the learner reads the file first (open every tab, then Start questions), then the file closes,
  key stats included, while the questions are asked. apply asks for reasoning with the facts (why, what would
  happen, compare); a stem that only looks a fact up ("How many...", "What is the name of...") ranks last.
- **Generator**: Gemini writes the file and a pool of 6-8 questions; code drops a stem that prints its key, a
  repeated choice or "all/none of the above", ranks down a key that is plainly the longest or that the file does not
  state, never ships two questions where one's key is printed in the other, stamps ids, `fileOpen` from the pin and
  the tier's starting position, and shuffles the choices. Fewer than two usable questions throws instead of padding.
  `config.difficulty`: easy shows the section note from the start; hard prefers wrong choices that are facts from
  elsewhere in the file. All tiers keep four choices.
- **Scripted path** keeps one try per question and its own Next, now scored on the answers only.
- **With the tutor**: no Next, a wrong tap marks only the learner's choice ("Not quite."; no key, no explanation),
  the explanation shows after a right answer. K (pre-reader) gets a picture-choice grid the tutor reads aloud; the
  scripted K path keeps `PreReaderSelfCheck`.

Every check whose choices can be said is `speech` with `acceptsGesture` (J14), `expectedAnswer` the key, and spoken
misses under the tap's ids. Misses (`factFileMiss`, every mode): `other_file_fact` (the choice is something the file
states elsewhere), `not_in_file`.

- **r1.** By reading: on apply the tutor pointed at the right choice by its content ("see which one mentions having
  so many different species", "which connects plants to moisture in the air", "how the basin spreads across the
  continent") in 9/60 stuck and lever replies; recall named the section in words without the lever ("think back to

- **Fix.** Guidance: never describe a choice by what it is about, never hint at the idea the answer rests on, never

  "floats or rises" lead 2/5. Guidance quotes both as forbidden.
- **r3.** No reply states a key, points at a choice by its content, or names a section without the lever. Section
