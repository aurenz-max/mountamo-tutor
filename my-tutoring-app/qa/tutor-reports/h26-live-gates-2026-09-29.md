## Findings

- **LB-16, letter-spotter `row_scan` was not on screen when announced (fixed).** `scanning` required `runner.canAttempt`,
  and after a wrong tap the item stays `checked` until Try again. The host message said "It is on screen now" and the
  tutor said "Notice how the rows are now highlighted" while nothing was drawn. The lever test pressed retry before
  looking, so it missed this. Now drawn from the pull (off once credited), the grid carries `data-lever="row-scan"`, and
  the test asserts both before Try again (LetterSpotter 45/45). No other primitive gates a lever's drawing on
  `canAttempt` (grep).
- **LB-20, knowledge-check marks (fixed).** The cue was drawn from the pull but marked `data-lever-cue`, and a dropped
  choice had only `data-dropped`. Both now also carry `data-lever` (KnowledgeCheck 74/74). This is the same class as LB-14.
  The saved run keeps its FAIL because its DOM count was taken live; `analyze_run.py` cannot change that.
- **LB-21, the second wrong spoken choice was never recorded (RP-2, handoff 27).** "Police badge" twice. The first was
  committed wrong (with no `miss`). The tutor answered the second with a coaching question, and the observer returned
  `none`. The 2nd-wrong auto-pull never had a second wrong, so the pull came from "I'm stuck". Filed against RP-2, not
  worked around. Re-run this one ladder after RP-2 ships.
- **LB-18, numeric choices lose every lever early (open, `/add-support-tiers`).** A number key withholds
  `cue_picture`, and `drop_far_choice` needs two untried choices. A 3-choice numeric item therefore has no lever after
  its first wrong, and a 4-choice one none after its second. So the 2nd-wrong auto-pull has nothing to pull on any
  numeric or drop-only item. Across all 20 saved KC problems, only `cue_picture` items keep a lever after two wrongs.
- **Leak read.** No answer word before a try in any run. cvc-speller says "cat" (dictation is the task), rhyme-studio
  names the two choices (the spoken choice is the task), and KC's hint ("a big red truck") names no choice.
- Run 4's tutor followed credit with "Now, who sat on the mat?", a comprehension question the item does not ask.
  Observation only.
