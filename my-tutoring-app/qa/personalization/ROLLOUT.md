# Student-interests rollout queue

Owning queue for carrying a student's interests (`students/{id}.interests`) into the primitives that render them. Slices 1-2 (persona read, counting-board triple) are in the [handoff](../HANDOFF-student-interests-themed-lessons-2026-09-16.md). Pull the top `open` row, close it here with its evidence, and update the WORKSTREAMS "Student interests" row in the same slice.

## How rows were ranked (audit 2026-09-16, read-only)

The manifest themes at most 2 component intents per lesson, or every component intent when the lesson has one objective (always true at PreK since the objective cap shipped 09-16). It changes words only. A themed intent is wasted when the chosen primitive cannot show the noun. A row is worth doing when these four conditions hold:

1. **Reach.** The primitive is served at PreK, K or G1.
2. **The theme is dropped today.** The noun comes from a closed enum or map (CLOSED), or the intent never reaches the prompt that writes the noun (OPEN-UNREAD).
3. **The theme is load-bearing.** The child counts, handles, hears or answers about the themed thing.
4. **Theming is safe.** The theme cannot change the target content.

Among rows that meet all four, the smaller change goes first.

**Reach sources:**
- **Student 9001** (the only K account): `students/9001/reviews`, 2026-02-26 to 2026-09-14. Distinct days are reported, not raw counts: the history includes developer bursts, e.g. base-ten-blocks has 873 reviews over 3 days.
- **K/G1 manifest picks:** 110 K and 40 G1 saved lessons under `qa/lesson-bench/`, `qa/lesson-coverage/` and `qa/topic-traces/`. 63 of the 110 K lessons are reruns of two topics, so the distinct-topic count (in parentheses) is the less biased figure.
- **Scripts:** kept in the session scratchpad only, not in the repo. Re-derive from the fields named above.

**Checking a slice:** `scripts/audit-intent-consumption.mjs` is not proof that a theme arrives. It only checks that the code references `ctx.intent`, so it passes array-grid and ordinal-line, whose noun-writing prompts never see the intent. Each row is verified by a live probe instead: a themed intent is sent in, and the probe checks that the noun appears in the rendered data AND in the tutor's lines. Row 1 turns `scripts/probe-counting-board-themed-objects.mjs` into a per-primitive probe, and the rows after it reuse that probe.

- **Real-object shape answers:** shape-sorter, di-shapes (code owns the object-to-shape answer), 3d-shape-explorer (an excavator is not one solid).
- **Literacy anchors:** the keywords in letter-sound-link and di-letter-sounds; the WORD_MENU and SENTENCE_MENU menus.

- **Adult reader:** take-home-activity.

## Decisions owed (user)

- **D1: interest-anchored lessons.** The voice block forbids an interest from changing component selection (`gemini-manifest.ts:134`). So a child who loves excavators meets the vehicle sims only when the lesson TOPIC is about vehicles. Under the lesson-entry principle, the route would be a Lesson Builder fill mode that proposes interest-anchored objectives. PreK-ready vehicle content exists today: fact-file (trash trucks) and how-it-works (the K picture-ordering path). The sims are not K-ready, and two of these gaps are bugs whatever the decision:
  - **dump-truck-loader:** its fixed density jobs override the prompt's K "full/empty" guidance (`gemini-dump-truck-loader.ts:399-403`), and it has no tutoring block.
  - **excavator-arm-simulator:** it always runs five fixed missions (`ExcavatorArmSimulator.tsx:168-214`), and it declares `jointControl` but never reads it (`:100`).
  - **hydraulics-lab:** its grade band enum stops at 3-5.
- **D2: theming assessment.** This decides row 8 and whether row 3's knowledge-check half themes the question stem or only the scenario.
- **D3: measure after rows 1-4.** In a batch of themed topic-traces, count how often a themed intent lands on a primitive from "Leave alone". At PreK every component is themed, so the count there is the share of the lesson that shows no theme. The user ruling allows no manifest rules, so the fix for a high count is more themeable primitives, not a selection rule.
